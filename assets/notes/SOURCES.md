# Banknote image sources

Images in `assets/notes/` were downloaded from
[Ron Wise's World Paper Money](http://banknote.ws/) catalog pages for Vietnam
(Pick numbers P-101 through P-124).

These are verified collector-reference scans of genuine Vietnamese banknotes
(front `*o.jpg` and reverse `*r.jpg`). Catalog page URLs are stored on each
variation in the app for attribution.
